# Project

## Name
Community Garden

## Summary
A volunteer-run neighborhood garden that shares planting space and teaches beginners.

## Goals
- Recruit 20 volunteers
- Plan a spring planting calendar

## Audience
Local residents and families

## Scope
### In scope
- Volunteer onboarding
- Planting schedule

### Out of scope
- Commercial produce sales

## Success criteria
- 20 active volunteers by spring

## Constraints
- Small volunteer budget
- Shared public space
